Who We Are?

CVRS is a computing vision and remote sensing lab funded by EVOC Intelligent Technology Company at Shenyang Jianzhu University, China, led by Prof. Yuan Shuai. Our research centers on Depth Estimation, Image Stitching, SLAM, and 3D point cloud processing.
